Camera access

SlingTab can watch for a circular hand gesture instead of a mouse drag.

Chrome will ask for permission. This page has to be a normal tab — the toolbar popup closes the instant the permission bubble takes focus, which cancels the request.
waiting for a hand… circle detected

The skeleton is the hand model's 21 landmarks. Raise index and middle only, then draw a circle — the pale line is the path the recogniser is accumulating, and the badge lights up when it closes. Change this to accept any hand shape in More settings.

The dashed rectangle is your screen. Everything inside it maps onto the whole browser window, so its edges are the edges of the display and its shape is your display's shape — which is why a circle drawn inside it stays a circle when the portal opens. A hand outside the rectangle is pinned to the nearest edge, so you never have to reach the corners of the picture to reach the corners of the screen.

  • Video is processed on your machine, frame by frame, and discarded.
  • Nothing is recorded, uploaded, or stored.
  • The camera only runs while the hand trigger is selected.
  • SlingTab reads hand landmarks — joint positions and which fingers are raised. It does not identify you, and no frame is kept.
  • The model runs locally, in WebAssembly, bundled with the extension. It works offline.
