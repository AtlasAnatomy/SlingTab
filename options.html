What that second one does
  • For the framed request only, SlingTab removes the destination's X-Frame-Options and Content-Security-Policy response headers. Those are the site's clickjacking protection. The rule is scoped to one tab, applies only to the framed sub-request, and is dropped the moment the preview loads, or on navigation, or by a five-second watchdog, whichever comes first.
  • The framed page cannot be clicked. Pointer events are off, forms and pop-ups are blocked, and it can never navigate your tab. Clickjacking needs a click to steal and there is none.
  • Framed sites appear logged out. Cookies marked SameSite=Lax are not sent to a cross-site frame.
  • Some sites will not frame whatever we do: a page served from its own service worker never hits the network, and a site whose own headers forbid framing third parties blocks the preview from the page you are standing on. Those fall back to the card below.

When a site will not frame, the disc shows a preview built from the destination's own og:image and theme colour. With no preview image, SlingTab composes a card: favicon, hostname and page title over the site's colour. That path works everywhere and strips nothing.
